## Our first wave of sponsors and partners

SatSummit brings together people building Earth observation technologies with those applying them to challenges in climate, humanitarian response, global development, natural resource management, and beyond. The event is not for profit, and support from sponsors and partners helps us bring that community together for two days of conversations, ideas, and connections.

### Welcome to our sponsors and partners

#### Silver Sponsor
[LGND](https://lgnd.ai/)

#### Bronze Sponsors
[Ai2](https://allenai.org/)

[GeoSTL](https://geostl.org/)

#### Community Sponsors
[Overture Maps Foundation](https://overturemaps.org/)

[NASA Lifelines](https://nasalifelines.org/)

#### Venue Partner
[The Post Building](https://www.thepoststl.com/)

#### Media Partner
[Geoawesome](https://geoawesome.com/)


Together, this first group reflects the range of perspectives we want represented at SatSummit: Earth observation and AI, open geospatial technology, humanitarian applications, and a strong connection to the geospatial community here in St. Louis.

That intersection is also part of why our sponsors are supporting the event. Ai2 describes SatSummit as “one of the few rooms where the people building Earth observation technology and the people applying it to global challenges sit at the same table.” For GeoSTL, bringing SatSummit to St. Louis is an opportunity to connect the region's geospatial ecosystem with new collaborators and ideas. And NASA Lifelines, a repeat sponsor, points to the importance of bringing the Earth science and humanitarian communities together around shared challenges.

Those connections are at the heart of SatSummit, and we're grateful to all of these organizations for helping make them possible.

### Join us in St. Louis

This is our first wave of sponsors and partners, with more announcements to come as we get closer to November.

SatSummit St. Louis takes place **November 18–19, 2026**, bringing together Earth observation experts, humanitarian organizations, industry leaders, climate practitioners, government agencies, researchers, and academics.

We offer Gold, Silver, and Bronze sponsorship packages designed for organizations of different sizes, along with opportunities to discuss custom sponsorship ideas.

Benefits include conference registrations, exhibit space, event visibility, social media promotion, and opportunities to connect with attendees throughout the event. Gold sponsors also receive the opportunity to help shape a breakout session topic in collaboration with the organizing team.

If your organization wants to support meaningful conversations around the future of Earth observation while connecting with leaders across the satellite ecosystem, we'd love to hear from you.

View the [sponsorship prospectus](https://satsummit.io/2026-st-louis-sponsor-prospectus.pdf) and contact us at info@satsummit.io to start the conversation.
