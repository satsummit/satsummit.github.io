SatSummit brings together people from across the satellite industry, Earth observation, global development, humanitarian response, research, government, and the broader geospatial community. Community support makes that possible: SatSummit is not a for-profit event, and sponsorship helps us create a space for substantive conversations, new connections, and the exchange of ideas across sectors.

## Our first wave of sponsors and partners

### LGND - Silver Sponsor

We're excited to welcome [LGND](https://lgnd.ai/) as a Silver Sponsor of SatSummit St. Louis.

Ai2 - Bronze Sponsor

Ai2 is joining SatSummit as a Bronze Sponsor and bringing a particular interest in the intersection of Earth observation technology and real-world applications.

“Ai2 is proud to sponsor SatSummit. It’s one of the few rooms where the people building Earth observation technology and the people applying it to global challenges sit at the same table. That intersection is what we built OlmoEarth for — open foundation models and an end-to-end platform that help partners turn Earth observation data into real decisions on the ground.”

GeoSTL - Bronze Sponsor

As we bring SatSummit to St. Louis for the first time, we're especially glad to have GeoSTL joining us as a Bronze Sponsor and helping connect the global SatSummit community with the region's geospatial ecosystem.

“GeoSTL is excited to sponsor SatSummit and welcome this global community to St. Louis. This is a meaningful opportunity to showcase our region’s geospatial talent and innovation. SatSummit’s focus on putting satellite data to work on global challenges aligns with GeoSTL’s mission, and we look forward to connecting our local ecosystem with new collaborators and ideas.”

Overture Maps Foundation - Community Sponsor

We're pleased to welcome Overture Maps Foundation as a Community Sponsor.

NASA Lifelines - Community Sponsor

NASA Lifelines joins us as a Community Sponsor, supporting the connections between Earth science and humanitarian communities that have long been central to SatSummit.

“SatSummit is a hugely important convening for the earth science and humanitarian communities to come together around big challenges, meet new collaborators, and chart a collective path forward.”

The Post Building - Venue Partner

We're also pleased to welcome The Post Building as an Institutional Partner and the home of SatSummit St. Louis on November 18–19.

Geoawesome - Media Partner

Geoawesome joins SatSummit St. Louis as our Media Partner, helping us share the conversations, people, and ideas surrounding this year's event with the wider geospatial community.

## Join us in St. Louis

This is just our first wave of supporters, with more SatSummit news coming soon.

SatSummit St. Louis takes place November 18–19, 2026. Tickets are available now, and sponsorship opportunities remain for organizations interested in supporting the event and connecting with a community spanning Earth observation, humanitarian organizations, industry, climate, government, and research.

We offer Gold, Silver, and Bronze sponsorship packages designed for organizations of different sizes, along with opportunities to discuss custom sponsorship ideas.

Benefits include conference registrations, exhibit space, event visibility, social media promotion, and opportunities to connect with attendees throughout the event. Gold sponsors also receive the opportunity to help shape a breakout session topic in collaboration with the organizing team.

If your organization wants to support meaningful conversations around the future of Earth observation while connecting with leaders across the satellite ecosystem, we'd love to hear from you.

View the [sponsorship prospectus](https://satsummit.io/2026-st-louis-sponsor-prospectus.pdf) and contact us at info@satsummit.io to start the conversation.
