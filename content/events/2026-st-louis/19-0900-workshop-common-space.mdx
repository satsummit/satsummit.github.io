Common Space is building a new kind of Earth observation infrastructure: high-resolution satellite imagery designed from the ground up as a shared resource for public good. In this interactive workshop, we’ll share our initial product architecture and invite the SatSummit community to help shape what comes next. Participants will pressure-test how users discover, task, access, analyze, and share imagery; explore workflows for emergencies and proactive collection; and identify where tools, APIs, AI, alerts, and integrations could make the greatest difference. Rather than presenting a finished product, we’ll bring early designs, assumptions, and open questions to the room. The goal is to leave with a clearer understanding of what Common Space should build, what it should integrate with rather than reinvent, and what a truly community-centered satellite data platform needs to work in the real world.

Workshop space is limited. Sign-up will happen in-person at registration.
