Thermal imagery shows what optical and radar can’t: whether a power plant is running, where a city is hottest, where a wildfire is spreading at night. With SatVu’s HotSat-2 now delivering high-resolution thermal data and HotSat-3 newly in orbit, that layer is more available than ever. Scott Herman, SatVu’s CTO, will open with a short overview of the constellation and what thermal can reveal including examples from industrial and facility monitoring. Then he’ll turn it over to you. This is a listening session: what problems could thermal help you solve, what would you need to use it, and where could SatVu and this community work together? Bring your use cases, your questions, and your wish lists.
