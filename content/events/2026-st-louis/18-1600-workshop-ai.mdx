This workshop explores the intersection of “explainable AI” and uncertainty quantification for tools that rely on satellite data + AI for decision-support. As it seems like the data science/computer science community is moving towards more “black box” like systems, how can the remote sensing science community play a role in evaluation, testing, and building tools for others to evaluate? Join us for a community discussion about what needs to be measured, why, what’s challenging about doing so, who’s having success, and how to communicate uncertainty in this new realm of agents.
