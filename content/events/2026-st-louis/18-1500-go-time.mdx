From the Ebola response to the Colombia-Venezuela earthquakes to the Nepal floods, the same cycle keeps repeating: the imagery could have changed everything if only it had reached the right hands in time. This session asks what it will actually take to break that cycle, so imagery shows up early enough to help save the day instead of just documenting it afterward
