How do you survive an institutional crisis? Reinvent yourself amid a changing landscape? And what's the role of imagery in your success and resilience — the same today, or changed along the way? This session sits down with leaders who've been through it: the funding cliffs, the mission pivots, the moments an organization had to become something different to keep going.
