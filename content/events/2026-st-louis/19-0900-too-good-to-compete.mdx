Why rivals are building one open, global map. Overture Maps Foundation tackles questions this community cares deeply about on a daily basis. When corporate members fund and govern an open dataset, whose priorities shape what gets mapped, and how well? How does Overture relate to OpenStreetMap and the volunteer mappers whose work underpins so much humanitarian response? Can an open map built largely by AI and corporate data sources reach the places that matter most for crisis response and development, where commercial incentives are weakest? Will Mortenson joins us to discuss how Overture works, where it's headed, and what a club-funded open data model can teach the rest of the geospatial world about sustaining public goods at scale.
