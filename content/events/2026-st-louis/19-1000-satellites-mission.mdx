A new generation of Earth observation operators is flipping the traditional build model: instead of designing a satellite and finding a market for it, they start with a problem on Earth and build the mission around solving it. This panel brings those operators together to talk about how that shift changes funding and design decisions, where being purpose-built creates real advantages, and where it creates real constraints.
