AI can detect, classify, predict, and analyze at scales that were unimaginable just a few years ago. But what happens when those capabilities aren't just a demo and influence decisions that affect people's lives? Practitioners share what happened when they actually put AI to work. Real outcomes, real stakes, real lessons learned.
