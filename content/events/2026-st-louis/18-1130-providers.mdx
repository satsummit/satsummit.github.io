Two panels, one conversation. On one side, the satellite builders and operators talking candidly about how they engage with humanitarian, research, and public-sector users, what's shifting in coverage and access, and the tradeoffs they navigate every day. On the other, the responders, researchers, and implementers putting that imagery to work with concrete stories from the field about what worked, what didn't, and where the gap between "technically possible" and "operationally usable" affects their work. Together, we'll surface the tensions, challenges, and opportunities that shape this relationship — and give each side a chance to speak directly to the other.
