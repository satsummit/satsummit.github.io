Part hackathon, part speed dating. Instead of a panel or a deck, we bring a blank Data Studio and build it in the room with attendees. We'll pitch a theme, break participants out and rotate them through small groups to source and vet datasets, tools, and workflows, then reconvene to stitch the pieces into a data studio by the end of the session. Everyone who contributes gets credited as a co-author of the resulting studio.

Workshop space is limited. Sign-up will happen in-person at registration.
