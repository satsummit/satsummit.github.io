A Data Studio is a package of case studies, tools, datasets, and how-to guides built around one humanitarian question. In this session, we build one live, focused on operational baselines: who and what is where before a crisis hits. Rotate through small groups to source and vet the best resources, trading notes with peers from across the satellite data and humanitarian communities along the way. No GIS expertise is needed. Participants will be credited as co-authors of the published resource.
