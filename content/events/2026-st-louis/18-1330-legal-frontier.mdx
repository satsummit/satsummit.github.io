Space law is racing to keep pace with a fast-moving industry. In this fireside chat, Kevin Pomfret joins us to unpack where the rules stand, where they're still being written, and what that means for anyone working with commercial and geospatial data. Expect a candid, wide-ranging conversation on where the law is headed, and what it means for everyone building on satellite data today.
