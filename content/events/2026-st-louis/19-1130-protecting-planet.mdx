Satellites, AI, and other emerging tools are reshaping how we track environmental change and respond to risk, but their real test is whether they reach the people most vulnerable to it. This session takes a systems view: people and planet are not separate problems, and the technologies that matter are the ones built with that intersection in mind. We'll explore what's actually working, what isn't, and what it takes to turn novel approaches into outcomes that protect both communities and the ecosystems they depend on.
