A hands-on workshop introducing Agents for the Planet, where participants will explore how environmental and conservation organizations can build trustworthy AI agents around their own expertise, data, and real-world workflows. Attendees will explore practical examples and leave with a clearer understanding of where agents can help, what it takes to use them responsibly, and how to identify a promising agentic workflow within their own organization. We will also share examples from our early partner pilots and invite attendees to help shape the approach.

Workshop space is limited. Sign-up will happen in-person at registration.
