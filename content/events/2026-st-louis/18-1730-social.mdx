Join us from 5:30pm to 8:30pm at City Museum, St. Louis's giant playground built from the city itself. Drinks are free for the first two hours, and small plates will be provided.

City Museum fills the 10-story, 600,000-square-foot former International Shoe Company warehouse on Washington Avenue downtown (750 N. 16th St.). Most of it is built from salvaged parts of the city, including old bridges, chimneys, construction cranes, factory conveyors, and bank vaults.
