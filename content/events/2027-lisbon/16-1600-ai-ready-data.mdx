Over the past decade, EO has converged on cloud-native formats and publication standards. AI workloads bring very different data access patterns: sequential reads, large batches, different chunking, and embeddings. How much does that change the way we store and publish data? Should AI-ready EO data live on a common global grid, or is native geometry plus good metadata enough? Do our existing formats and standards hold up, or do they need rethinking?
