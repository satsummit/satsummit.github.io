Over the past decade, EO has converged on cloud-native formats and publication standards. AI workloads bring very different data access patterns, sequential reads, large batches, different chunking, and embeddings. To what extent does that change how we store and publish data? Should AI-ready EO data live on a common global grid, or is native geometry plus good metadata enough? Do our existing formats and standards hold up, or do they need rethinking?
