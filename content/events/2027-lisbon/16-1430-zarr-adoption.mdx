Zarr has been gaining a lot of traction in geospatial. Major data providers are exploring publishing their archives in Zarr, and others are adopting native Zarr, GeoZarr, or virtual Zarr over existing archives. That momentum sits mostly upstream, with providers, platforms, and the groups building tooling and raising awareness. Downstream, users face practical questions: which flavor to target, what do they get out of Zarr's adoption, whether their tools are ready, and whether adoption requires reworking existing pipelines. What does the sector need to do to carry adoption the rest of the way?
