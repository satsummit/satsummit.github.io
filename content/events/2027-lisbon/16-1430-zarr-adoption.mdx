Zarr is gaining real traction in geospatial. Major data providers are exploring publishing their archives in Zarr, while others are adopting native Zarr, GeoZarr, or virtual Zarr over existing archives. Most of that momentum sits upstream. Downstream, users face practical questions: what they gain, whether their tools are ready, and whether they'll need to rework existing pipelines. What does the sector need to do to carry adoption the rest of the way?
