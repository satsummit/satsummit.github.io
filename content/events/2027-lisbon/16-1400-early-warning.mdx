Early warning is moving from describing hazards to triggering action, whether that means releasing humanitarian funds before a flood or adapting to an ocean system that may be approaching a tipping point. Forecasters, humanitarian responders, and data providers discuss where EO makes those triggers trustworthy, and what's still missing to make them work at scale.
