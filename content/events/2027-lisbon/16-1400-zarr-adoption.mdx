Zarr is gaining real traction in geospatial. Major data providers are publishing archives in Zarr, and others are adopting native Zarr, GeoZarr, or virtual Zarr over existing archives. Most of that momentum sits upstream. Downstream, users face practical questions: which flavour to target, what they gain, whether their tools are ready, and how much of their pipelines they'll need to rework. What will it take to carry adoption the rest of the way?
