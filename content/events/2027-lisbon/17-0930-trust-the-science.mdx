title: "Can We Still Trust the Science When Agents Do the Work?"
type: Panel
date: 2027-03-17 09:30

---

AI and agentic workflows are reshaping how people get insights from EO data. Foundation models, multi-tool chains, and agents make traditional reproducibility hard, and sometimes impossible. Open-science advocates, AI practitioners, data stewards, and the people relying on the results discuss how we keep the open-science values this sector was built on.
