Getting environmental sensor and model data has never been easier, and it has rarely been more confusing. New platforms, portals, APIs, and services have multiplied in the past few years. Is that a healthy sign of innovation, or a hidden tax on adoption? And what would a good landscape look like in five years: fewer platforms, or better ways to find your way among many?
