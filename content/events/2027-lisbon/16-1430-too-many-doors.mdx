Getting environmental sensor and model data has never been easier, and yet it has rarely been more confusing. In the past few years, new platforms, portals, APIs, and services have multiplied. Is this proliferation a healthy sign of innovation, or a hidden tax on adoption? What would a 'good' landscape look like in five years? Fewer platforms, or better ways to find your way among many?
