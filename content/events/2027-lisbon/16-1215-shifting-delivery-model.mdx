Two shifts are reshaping how EO data reaches the people who use it. Cloud-native geospatial has moved from emerging trend to the default way data is stored and distributed. At the same time, AI and agentic systems are changing how people and machines find, access, and consume that data. Data providers, product builders, and users discuss what these shifts open up, and what we still have to solve.
