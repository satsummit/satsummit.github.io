Two shifts are reshaping how Earth Observation data reaches its users. Cloud-native geospatial has moved from an emerging trend to being the default way data is stored and distributed. AI and agentic systems are changing how people and machines find, access, and consume that data. This panel looks at what these shifts mean for the sector: the opportunities they open up and the challenges we still have to solve.
