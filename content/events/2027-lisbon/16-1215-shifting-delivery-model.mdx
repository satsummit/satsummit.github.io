Two shifts are reshaping how Earth observation (EO) data reaches its users. Cloud-native geospatial has gone from emerging trend to the default way data is stored and distributed. Meanwhile, AI and agentic systems are changing how people and machines find, access, and use that data. What do these shifts mean for the sector: the opportunities they open up, and the challenges we still have to solve?
