title: "Funding Innovation at the Speed of Change"
type: Panel
date: 2027-03-16 15:30
room: 
---

Innovation in EO and AI now moves in months, while many of Europe's main funding instruments still run on multi-year proposal cycles. By the time a proposal is written, evaluated, and contracted, the state of the art has often moved on. New models are emerging alongside the traditional ones: mission-driven agencies, challenge prizes, co-investment, anchor-customer commitments, and blended public-private capital. Funders, founders, and investors discuss which mechanisms actually speed things up, and how to shift from funding proposals to funding progress.
