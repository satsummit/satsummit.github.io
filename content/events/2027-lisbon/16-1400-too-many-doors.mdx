date: 2027-03-16 14:00

Getting environmental sensor and model data has never been easier, and yet it has rarely been more confusing. New platforms, portals, APIs, and services keep multiplying. Is this a healthy sign of innovation, or a hidden tax on adoption? Data users, public programmes, and platform builders discuss what a good landscape would look like in five years: fewer platforms, or better ways to find your way among many.
