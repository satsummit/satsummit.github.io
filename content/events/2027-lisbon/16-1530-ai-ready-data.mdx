Over the past decade, EO has converged on cloud-native formats and publication standards. AI workloads bring very different access patterns: sequential reads, large batches, different chunking, and embeddings. Does that change how we store and publish data? Should AI-ready EO data live on a common global grid, or is native geometry plus good metadata enough? Standards stewards, model builders, and data providers test whether our current formats hold up.
