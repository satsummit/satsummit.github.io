Seamus Geraty is the community tools lead for NASA Lifelines, passionate about leveraging geospatial technology for problem solving in the Global Health and Humanitarian space. Seamus has a background in GIS and Global Health and serves as a Technical Consultant for DevGlobal in their geospatial practice area supporting projects in GIS research and application, Earth observation & remote sensing, machine learning, and creative science communication.
