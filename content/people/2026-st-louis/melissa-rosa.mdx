Melissa Rosa is an independent consultant specializing in Earth observation and geospatial data for public good. She helps organizations build partnerships and adoption strategies that expand the use of satellite imagery and geospatial products while demonstrating their value for climate resilience, environmental sustainability, and humanitarian response. At Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team (HOT), she leads imagery provider engagement and partnerships for OpenAerialMap, increasing access to openly licensed imagery for humanitarian mapping and disaster response.
