Bill Greer is Co-Founder of Common Space, a nonprofit building the first community-governed, openly licensed Earth observation satellite constellation for humanitarian use. Common Space aims to put high-resolution satellite imagery in the hands of the communities, responders, and civil society groups who need it most. Bill has spent his career making geospatial data easier to use. Bill has held leadership positions in some of the most influential organizations across the Spatial and remote sensing industries including GeoCommons, Esri, Maxar, and Albedo, and Premise Data, including field deployments in humanitarian settings in Kurdistan and Mali. He is based in Denver, Colorado.
