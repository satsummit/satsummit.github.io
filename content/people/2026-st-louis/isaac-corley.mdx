Isaac Corley is Director of AI/ML Research at Taylor Geospatial, where he leads research in GeoAI, Earth observation, and geospatial foundation models. His work spans research through production, with a focus on building scalable, reliable AI systems for global geospatial applications.

Previously, Isaac built the RasterFlow platform at Wherobots, served as a Principal Investigator on the IARPA SMART program at BlackSky, and deployed computer vision systems for renewable energy inspection at Zeitview. He also maintains TorchGeo, a widely used PyTorch library for geospatial deep learning.

Isaac holds a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Texas at San Antonio and regularly speaks on geospatial AI, machine learning, and open-source research.
