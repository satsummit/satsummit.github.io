As Director of Commercialization at Taylor Geospatial, Nick Reinke closes the gap where most breakthroughs die—between innovative research and market-ready solutions—getting transformative tools into the hands of those who need them.

Nick brings over 20 years of leadership in sustainable agriculture, agtech, and ecosystem markets—the real-world expertise needed to unlock geospatial AI breakthroughs for the digital public good. He comes to Taylor Geospatial after a successful tenure as CEO of HabiTerre, an ag and geospatial technology company specializing in environmental and productivity modeling for food and agriculture. HabiTerre was a 2022 recipient of startup funding from Arch Grants, which brought Nick to St. Louis, where he established himself as a key figure in the ecosystem. He earned recognition as one of St. Louis Business Journal’s 40 Under 40 in 2024 and as an emerging leader on St. Louis Magazine’s Business 500 list in 2026.

Leveraging his background in ag finance, soil health, and environmental and carbon market development, Nick knows how to navigate the intersection of data, sustainability, and commercialization to create lasting impact.

If you’re bringing geospatial technology to market, investing in it, or pushing for its advancement, Nick wants to hear from you. Reach out to share what you’re working on, and let’s explore how Taylor Geospatial can help you take it further.
