Micah Farfour is the Special Adviser for Remote Sensing with Amnesty International's Evidence Lab, where she leads geospatial and satellite imagery analysis in support of human rights investigations. With over ten years of experience in open-source investigations, she specializes in the use of earth observation data, geospatial analytics, and multi-source data integration to document and verify human rights abuses.
