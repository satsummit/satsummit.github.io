Elizabeth Foughty is Co-Founder and Chief Business Officer of Orbital Sentry, a deep tech imaging startup building geosynchronous satellite systems for climate risk intelligence and disaster response. She brings 20+ years of go-to-market expertise spanning insurance, remote sensing, and AI at organizations such as NASA, Moody's RMS, Planet Labs, and CAPE Analytics (Moody's).

At Orbital Sentry, Elizabeth leads commercial strategy and partnership development, translating cutting-edge satellite technology into scalable solutions for organizations on the front lines of climate risk. Her deep domain expertise in re/insurance and Earth observation uniquely positions her to bridge the gap between advanced sensing capabilities and the markets that need them most.
