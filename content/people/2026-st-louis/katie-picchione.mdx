Katie Picchione has been working at the intersection of technology and disasters since 2018, with a special focus on applications of remote sensing in emergency management. Her current interests include validation of imagery-based geospatial disaster data and system-level analysis of data product development. As a volunteer, Katie is part of the Common Space Satellite Task Force (Space Team!) and has advised the GISCorps PhotoMappers project since 2021. She previously held a research position with the Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief Systems group at MIT Lincoln Laboratory and served as an Emergency Management Specialist within the FEMA Response Geospatial Office. She holds a bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering from Worcester Polytechnic Institute and a master's in Technology and Policy from MIT. In her free time, Katie rings tower bells at Washington National Cathedral and is part of the North American Guild of Change Ringers.
