Katie Picchione has been working at the intersection of technology and disasters since 2018, with a special focus on applications of remote sensing in emergency management. She is currently the Response Coordination Lead for the NASA Disasters Response Coordination System (DRCS). She previously served as an Emergency Management Specialist at FEMA and held a research position with the Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief Systems group at MIT Lincoln Laboratory. She holds a bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering from Worcester Polytechnic Institute and a master's in Technology and Policy from MIT.

In a volunteer capacity, Katie is also an advisor for the GISCorps/NAPSG PhotoMappers project and recently provided technical advice to Common Space as a member of the Satellite Team. In her free time, Katie rings tower bells at Washington National Cathedral and is part of the North American Guild of Change Ringers. Opinions expressed are her own and do not represent her opinions as a Federal employee or that of NASA.
