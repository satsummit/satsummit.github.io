Andrea Steffke is Manager of Special Projects at Carbon Mapper, leading strategic initiatives that turn satellite emissions data into actionable insight for government agencies and partners. Her work spans proposal development, cross-functional project delivery, and growing the organization's special projects function.

Andrea has over 20 years of experience leading, managing, and executing remote sensing projects across research organizations, government agencies, and the private sector. She specializes in helping organizations unlock the value of Earth Observation data to support environmental protection and climate risk mitigation.

She holds a Ph.D. in Geology and Geophysics from the University of Hawai'i at Manoa, an M.S. in Geology from the University of Alaska Fairbanks, and a B.S. in Geology from the College of Charleston.
