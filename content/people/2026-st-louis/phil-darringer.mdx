Phil Darringer is Staff Product Manager at Wherobots, driving innovation in spatial analytics and geospatial AI. He previously led product strategy at Kinetica, advancing real-time spatial and temporal analytics, and held senior product management roles at HERE Technologies, Medio, RSA (EMC), and Oracle. A Stanford-trained electrical engineer and inventor on multiple U.S. patents, Phil combines deep technical expertise with a passion for transforming location data into actionable intelligence.
