Megan Gallagher is an Industry Principal with Pixxel Space. She received her BS at Colorado School of Mines in Geophysical Engineering, and MS at Boise State University in Geophysics. She has previously worked at NV5 Geospatial as a Sales Engineer specializing in many modalities of remote sensing technology, including LiDAR, SAR, multispectral, and hyperspectral imaging. She enjoys seeing just how far you can go with data fusion techniques in terrestrial remote sensing.
