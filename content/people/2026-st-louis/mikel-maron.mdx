Mikel Maron leads product and operations at Earth Genome, applying AI, science and design to put Earth data in the right hands at the right time, taking on today's biggest nature and climate challenges. His background includes leading corporate social responsibility at Mapbox and elevating open mapping in the federal government as a Presidential Innovation Fellow. He has an extensive track record in community driven data, notably founding Map Kibera and the Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team, alongside his years of service on the OSM Foundation Board.
