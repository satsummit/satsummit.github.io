Rhiannan Price is Co-Founder of Common Space, a nonprofit building an independent, community-governed humanitarian satellite constellation for open, public-benefit Earth observation. She previously founded Maxar's Open Data Program and led NASA Lifelines, and has advised NASA's Applied Sciences Advisory Committee and the ICC Office of the Prosecutor. A Taylor Geospatial Humanitarian Fellow-in-Residence and 2025 Kluz PeaceTech Prize recipient, she holds an M.A. in International Human Rights from the University of Denver's Josef Korbel School. A former Peace Corps Volunteer, Rhiannan has lived in Uganda, Tanzania, and Dominica and speaks four languages.
