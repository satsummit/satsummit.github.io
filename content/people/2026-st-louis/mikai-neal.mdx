Mikai Neal is a senior Communication Studies major, a Board of Regents Scholar, and four-year collegiate soccer player at Harris-Stowe State University, where she was recently named to the 2026 Academic All-Conference Women's Soccer Team. An accomplished student leader, she serves as Director of Public Relations for the Student Government Association.

Mikai is also building an exciting career at the intersection of communications and geospatial technology. She recently completed an internship as a Geospatial Analyst with T-Kartor US and has participated in the HBCU Immersion in GEOINT Program, the Esri Federal GIS Conference, and the Esri User Conference. She was also a featured speaker at the American Geographical Society's Geography 2050 event last year.

A St. Louis native, Mikai brings together leadership, communications, and geospatial science with a commitment to connecting people, expanding opportunities, and making a positive impact in her community.
