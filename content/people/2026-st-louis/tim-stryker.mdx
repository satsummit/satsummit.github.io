Tim Stryker is the Principal of Hudson Passage, LLC, a consulting firm advising clients on science and technology policy and partnerships for a more secure and sustainable world. His career spans more than three decades in leadership roles at multiple federal agencies, White House offices, and international coordination groups. His primary areas of expertise are Earth observations, science & technology and space policy, and international collaboration.

Mr. Stryker’s appointments and awards include the Presidential Management Fellowship; Senior Fellow, Council of Excellence in Government; White House Office of Science and Technology Policy Superior Service Award; and performance awards at multiple federal civil and defense agencies. He serves on the Board of Directors of AmericaView, a nationwide consortium of universities empowering remote sensing education, and served on the Governance Board of the Common Space international humanitarian satellite mission. Mr. Stryker also serves on the U.S. National Academies of Science, Engineering, and Medicine’s Standing Committee on Earth Observations, Data, and Information Systems. He is also a Senior Non-Resident Fellow at the International Computer Science Institute affiliated with the University of California-Berkeley.
