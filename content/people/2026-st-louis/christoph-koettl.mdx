Christoph Koettl is a reporter on the Visual Investigations team of The New York Times, where he examines photos, videos and satellite images in search of hidden clues that reveal new insights about current events. He has two decades of experience using innovative tools and methodologies for reporting and research, focusing on violations of human rights and the laws of war. Before he joined The Times in 2017, he worked for Amnesty International. His specialty is geospatial analysis and using satellite images to tell stories.
