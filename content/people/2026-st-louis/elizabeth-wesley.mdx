Dr. Elizabeth Wesley is a Data Scientist in the World Resources Institute's Ross Center for Sustainable Cities, where she uses satellite remote sensing and geospatial analysis to study urban heat and identify opportunities to make cities more climate-resilient. Her research focuses on translating high-resolution environmental data into actionable information for urban planning, policy, and investment decisions. She is particularly interested in the concept of "good enough" data: understanding what scale and level of detail are actually needed to generate useful information and drive meaningful change.

A central theme of Elizabeth's work is data equity: developing open-source data, methods, and tools that make actionable information accessible to cities and communities, including those without access to high-resolution data or extensive technical resources. At WRI, she contributes to the Cool Cities Lab—an open-access platform that helps cities understand and address extreme heat—and collaborates with research and technology partners to advance the use of geospatial data for urban climate applications.
