Andrew Pawloski is the CTO of Element 84 where he has spent over a decade building infrastructure projects at NASA, NOAA, USGS, and commercial Earth Observation data providers and data users. He is currently an architect in the project office for NASA's Multisource Integrated Observatory (MIO) where he is working on platform technologies to enable effective multi-source data integration to stimulate new opportunities and extract added value from mission observations.
