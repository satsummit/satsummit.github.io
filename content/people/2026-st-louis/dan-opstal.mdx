At USGS, Dan Opstal helps lead the National Land Imaging Program, home to Landsat. Previously, through the Civil Applications Committee, he worked where the civilian, commercial, defense, and intelligence communities meet, bringing remote-sensing capabilities to disasters, wildfires, and environmental change.
