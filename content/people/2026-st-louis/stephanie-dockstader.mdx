Stephanie Dockstader is Director of Imagery and Remote Sensing Solutions at Esri, where she leads strategy at the intersection of Earth observation, GIS, and artificial intelligence. She specializes in transforming data from satellites, drones, and other sensors into actionable insight that supports real-world decision-making.

Her work focuses on bridging cutting-edge science with operational use, helping organizations move from data collection to data curation in support of continuous monitoring and predictive analysis. Dockstader is known for making complex geospatial technologies accessible to both technical and executive audiences, enabling leaders to implement sensor data for mission-critical outcomes and long-term strategic planning. With a career spanning both the scientific and production sides of remote sensing, she brings expertise in imagery analysis, sensor data integration, and geospatial workflows. She has worked closely with research institutions, government agencies, and commercial organizations to translate advanced Earth observation capabilities into practical applications at scale.
