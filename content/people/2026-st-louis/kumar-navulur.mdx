Dr. Navulur is CEO of Synspective USA, Inc. and it's parent company Synspective Inc. designs, builds, and operates a constellation of X band SAR satellites. He has over 30+ years experience in the commercial remote sensing industry. Dr. Navulur received his PhD from Purdue University and was recognized as 2019 Distinguished Purdue ABE Alumnus. Dr. Navulur is a member of board of directors of Open Geospatial Consortium and Common Space. His interests include satellite technology, use of AI/ML, GenAI and Agentic AI technologies for the entire life cycle of earth observation satellites.
