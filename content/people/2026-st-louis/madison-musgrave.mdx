Madison Musgrave is an Industry Solutions Specialist on Esri’s Public Safety team, where she serves as Information and Data Manager for the Esri Disaster Response Program (DRP), a 24x7 global operation that has supported communities in crisis for more than 25 years. In her role, Madison helps organizations in the response phase of a disaster find, access, and apply the data and GIS technology they need to make faster, better-informed decisions. She leads engagement with technology partners to make critical data more accessible to those working in the field and creates and maintains public-facing GIS tools that support the full emergency management cycle.
