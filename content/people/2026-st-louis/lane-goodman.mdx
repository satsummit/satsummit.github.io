Lane is a UI/UX Designer at Development Seed. He specializes in both web design and organizational communications. He is passionate about designing for new technologies and their ability to increase awareness of and advocacy for global development challenges.

Before joining Development Seed, Lane worked as a communications officer at Results for Development, an international development NGO, providing design, communications strategy and usability guidance for web platforms. He also served as a public health educator in Guinea with the US Peace Corps, where he worked on malaria prevention, reproductive health education and infrastructure management.

Lane holds a B.F.A in photography and a B.A. in anthropology, both from Washington University in St. Louis.
