Andrew Howe leads partnership development on the OlmoEarth program at the Allen Institute for AI (Ai2). Throughout his career, he has worked to bridge technology and international development, focusing on democratizing access. This work has included crowdsourced data collection, country-level program strategy with multilateral institutions, gender inclusion and geospatial applications to combat illegal fishing with Skylight. In his role with OlmoEarth, he helps liaise between state-of-the-art earth observation AI research and applications to support food security, community resilience and ecosystem health.

Andrew grew up in North Dakota, spending summers working on his grandfather’s farm, and holds a master’s degree in International Development from the University of Oregon where he focused on agriculture as rural development.
