Quito Tsui is a PhD Student in Environmental Social Sciences at Stanford University where she focuses on technology use in complex deployment contexts, and the significance of the built and physical environment in sustainable transitional justice efforts. She is currently working to understanding the ethical stakes of technology use as part of evidentiary practices in international legal contexts. Prior to Stanford, Quito advised and produced original research for humanitarian organisations, INGOs and civil society organisations on a range of subject matter including biometrics, digital identity and AI. She holds an MPhil in Development Studies from the University of Cambridge.
