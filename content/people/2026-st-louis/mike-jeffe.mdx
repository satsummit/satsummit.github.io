Mike Jeffe is the Director of Business Development, AI & Big Tech at EarthDaily, where he focuses on the growing intersection of Earth observation and geospatial AI. He brings more than 15 years of experience across the EO industry, including roles at Vantor, AWS, Climate Corporation and DigitalGlobe spanning satellite imagery, geospatial standards, GEOINT, and AI-enabled workflows.
