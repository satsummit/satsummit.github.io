Chris Newton is a senior early warning analyst covering resources, infrastructure, and conflict at the International Crisis Group. His recent work there has covered the strait of Hormuz, weaponised starvation in Gaza, and US humanitarian policy. He previously worked in the humanitarian sector on famine early warning, including with the World Food Programme, FAO, and other organisations. He is a Visiting Fellow at the Feinstein International Center at Tufts University.
