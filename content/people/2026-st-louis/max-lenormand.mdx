Max Lenormand is a geospatial data scientist, podcaster & video producer, telling stories about satellite imagery and geospatial data. After working as a geospatial data scientist on flood modelling (ICEYE), cocoa & coffee tree mapping (Satelligence) and 3D vegetation monitoring (Overstory) and cloud compute infrastructure for large scale geospatial analysis (Fused) he moved back to telling stories about data & people. He has hosted and run the 'Minds Behind Maps' podcast and runs the 'Max Lenormand' channel on YouTube and worked with organizations like Radiant Earth to help communicate their work to a broader audience.
