## What it is

**SatSummit** brings together the people developing satellite and Earth observation technology and those using it to address real-world challenges, for two days of presentations and in-depth conversations.

## When and where

16–17 March 2027 at the [**Centro Cultural de Belém (CCB)**](https://www.ccb.pt/en/), in Lisbon, Portugal.

## The venue

SatSummit takes place in the **Congress and Conference Centre** of the Centro Cultural de Belém, on the banks of the Tagus river, next to the Jerónimos Monastery and the Monument to the Discoveries.

- **Almada Negreiros room (Room 6):** our main stage, on level 2, seating up to 300 people. It is accessible for people with reduced mobility.
- **Amália Rodrigues (Room 13) and Lopes-Graça (Room 14):** our breakout rooms, on level 2, each seating up to 100 people. Both open onto a terrace overlooking the river, where coffee breaks will take place, weather permitting.
- **Vitorino Nemésio room (Room 4):** where lunch is served, on level 1, with a large terrace overlooking the river and the Monument to the Discoveries.

**Social events:** the venue will be announced soon.

## How to get to the venue

The Centro Cultural de Belém is located at Praça do Império, 1449-003 Lisboa, Portugal, about 20 minutes from central Lisbon and 30 minutes from the airport.

### By public transport

- **Train:** Cais do Sodré – Cascais line, get off at **Belém** station.
- **Tram:** line **15**.
- **Bus:** lines **714, 727, 728, 729** and **751**.
- **Ferry:** river connections to the **Belém** terminal.

### By taxi or ride-hailing app

Ask to be dropped off at the Centro Cultural de Belém, Praça do Império.

### Parking

The CCB has two car parks: Park 1 (Rua Bartolomeu Dias) and Park 2 (Avenida da Índia), open every day from 8:00am to midnight, with a single daily rate of €4. Payment is in cash at the machine on the ground floor of Park 2, next to the lift, or with Via Verde. Two spaces for charging electric vehicles are available on the ground floor of Park 1.

## Accommodation

There are no room blocks or codes for SatSummit. You can stay either in **Belém**, within walking distance of the venue, or around **Cais do Sodré**, in central Lisbon, which has a direct train to Belém (about 10 minutes on the Cascais line). Cais do Sodré can be lively at night; if you prefer somewhere a little quieter but still close by, nearby **Chiado** is a short walk away.

### Is the venue wheelchair accessible?

Yes. The main room and the Congress Centre are accessible for people with reduced mobility. If you are arriving by car, please use the car park on the river side, accessed from Avenida da Índia (Park 2, Floor 0). Should you need any adjustments to make this event accessible to you, please reach out to us at [**info@satsummit.io**](mailto:info@satsummit.io).

### Is there a Code of Conduct in place?

Yes, there is. In order to offer a positive and safe environment for all attendees, we ask that you review the **[Code of Conduct](/code-of-conduct)**.

## Fulfillment policy

Please note that all ticket sales are final within 10 days of the event. Unfortunately, we cannot offer refunds during this period except under extraordinary circumstances, such as illness or airline-related issues. Should you encounter such a situation, we encourage you to reach out to us directly to discuss your situation at [info@satsummit.io](mailto:info@satsummit.io).

If your organisation has purchased a ticket but you can no longer attend, tickets are transferable to another individual within your organisation. Please contact us if you need assistance with transferring your ticket.

We appreciate your understanding and look forward to your participation at the conference.
